              PRINTKUROX HOSTEL STATION SETUP

Welcome to the PrintKurox Smart Printing Network!

HOW TO SET UP YOUR STATION IN 1 MINUTE:
1. Make sure your printer is plugged in via USB and turned ON.
2. Double-click "PrintKurox.exe".
3. Select your assigned Hostel Block or Shop Name from the list.
4. Enter your Pickup Spot (e.g., Room 102, Ground Floor).
5. Pick your printer from the dropdown (e.g., EPSON L3210 Series).
6. Create your 4-digit Admin PIN (used to log in to your dashboard).
7. Keep "Start automatically with Windows" checked.
8. Click "Register".

That's all! Your printer is now fully operational and listening for 
student print jobs. Documents will print cleanly and automatically.

FEATURES IN "PrintKurox.exe":
- Pure Black & White Enforcement: B&W orders print with ZERO color ink.
- Live Status & Spooler: Real-time print queue and connection log.
- Auto-Start Toggle: Easily enable or disable Windows boot startup anytime.
- Web Admin Portal: 1-click button to open your earnings dashboard.
- Print Test Page: Verify printer connectivity with a single click.
- Clean Uninstall: Built-in uninstaller button cleanly purges all services and startup shortcuts if needed.

YOUR DASHBOARD & EARNINGS:
